About Me

I am a 4th BS-MS student at Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Kolkata, pursuing major in physical sciences and minor in computer science.

I am a very curious person and find nearly every domain in science interesting. I have explored different domains ranging from computational biophysics, molecular dynamics, non-linear dynamics and use of machine learning in it. But with time, I experienced some extra attraction to human brain dynamics and consciousness. Reason behind this interest is more philosophy than physics. Now I want to work and explore in this domain later in MS and PhD.

My coursework covers fundamental topics of physics like classical and quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, non-linear dynamics, optics and wave dynamics etc. I also studied linear algebra, real analysis, mathematical methods, probability & stistics, machine learning, data science, computer architecture etc. My technical expertise extends into programming and algorithms. I am proficient in C,C++, Python, Julia and Fortran.

My Interest

Theoretical neuroscience: I am very interested to explore how emergent properties of consciousness and intelligence arise from neural architectures and distinct brain regions co-process signals to generate dynamic states. I view brain as a complex physical system that could be understood in terms of waves, states and interactions.

Neuromorphic computing: I am interested in brain mimicking hardware to build energy and time efficient complex information processing devices. As a physics student, I am intriguid by the idea of using physical systems to emulate biological synapses and neurons directly in the hardware.

Brain computer interface: I want to study on advanced devices and systems that enable human to connect with devices using high bandwidth data channel. As I believe that in future artificial intelligence would also be a part of human cognition and mind.

AI for science: Applying advanced machine learning algorithms to automate scientific discovery and theoretical physics derivation.

Overview:

My principle focus during the summer internship was to develop and refine custom high performance computing software in C++ and Python language. The primary goal was to perform calculations for the biophysical properties of water in the hydration layer using high frequency molecular simulation trajectories (from simulation software like GROMACS).
